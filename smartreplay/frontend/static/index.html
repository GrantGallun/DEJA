Controls & focus

The replay opens passive: on top of the game, with the game still taking your input. Pick how you take control of the player and give it back.

Input mode

How far the seek buttons and seek keys jump.

Hotkeys

Select a shortcut and press the new keys. Tab cancels; the ✕ button unbinds.

Global hotkeys

Work everywhere, even while the game has focus. They need Ctrl, Alt or Shift plus a letter, digit or F-key.

Player keys

Work only while the player has controls, so plain keys are fine.

Mouse only mode turns player keys off. They are kept for when you switch back.

Size & position

Where the player first appears. You can still drag and resize it while it has controls (not in Keyboard only mode); that lasts until the player restarts or these settings change.
